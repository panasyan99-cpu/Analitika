# Supplier Order Specification 1.10.4

## Stone navigation

`Sapphire`, `Ruby`, `Moissanite`, `Topaz`, `Green Stones`, `Other Stones`.

The navigation bucket never replaces the concrete stone stored in the order row or supplier Excel.

## Pearl navigation

`White`, `Grey`, `Pink`, `Black`, `Baroque`.

Sea and Round pearls are excluded. Unresolved generic pearl labels are not assigned to an invented colour and do not enter the supplier order.

## Category structure

Every visible bucket retains the standard set categories: top, medium, weak and zero.
